# Code of Conduct

camtrapEvents welcomes constructive participation from people of every
background and level of experience.

Be respectful, patient and specific. Discuss ideas and evidence without
personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention,
deliberate intimidation and the publication of another person's private
information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions
that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to winsamuel@hotmail.com. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible. Anyone involved
in a report must disclose conflicts of interest and step aside when needed.

This code applies in the repository and in other public spaces where someone is
representing the project.
